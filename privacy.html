Camping Morocco Privacy Policy

Updated for V4: 30 October 2026

Camping Morocco is a campsite discovery and community information service for Morocco. This policy explains the information processed by the app and its backend service.

Information we process

  • Account information: display name, email address, password hash, verification and security metadata, and optional communications preferences.
  • Community content and activity: reviews, reports, campsite corrections or suggestions, contribution records and photos you choose to upload. Submissions are held for moderation before public publication. Approved reviews and photos may be shown publicly with an associated display name.
  • Favorites: in the V4 offline-first directory, favorites are saved on your device and accessed after sign-in; they do not automatically sync across devices. Older account-based features may keep remote favorite records.
  • Location when you request nearby results: V4 offline-first proximity calculations run on your device using your location and bundled campsite coordinates. This local search does not send your position to our API. Older online nearby functions, where available and actively invoked, may transmit coordinates for that request. We do not intentionally store them in your user profile.
  • Technical service data: limited server and security logs may be processed for operations, security and diagnostics.

How we use information

We process data to provide sign-in and account security, store and display moderated submissions, support local favorites and nearby calculations, send transactional account messages, respect optional communications preferences, prevent abuse and maintain reliability.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by the infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate the app, including hosting, database, storage and email-delivery providers, subject to their terms and appropriate safeguards. Third-party maps and external links may involve separate network requests when you use them.

Retention and account deletion

Delete your account using the account section inside the app or the external account-deletion instructions. Account deletion removes your sign-in identity and associated server-side content such as reviews, reports, uploaded photos and pending corrections. Local device favorites and settings may remain until you clear the app's local data or uninstall it. Campsite facts already accepted into the public directory may remain without a link to your identity.

Security

Production services use HTTPS, hashed passwords, authenticated access, rate limits and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may browse public campsite information without creating an account. Location permission is requested only for location-based features. Optional communications can be enabled or disabled independently.

Children

Camping Morocco is a general travel-information service and is not intended as a dedicated child-directed service. Users should follow applicable platform and supervision requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions contact bisapp.ink@outlook.com. This policy may be updated as functionality or legal requirements change. Offline maps are not guaranteed beyond previously viewed cached tiles.
