Delete your Camping Morocco account

You can remove your Camping Morocco account and its associated server-side contributions using one of the methods below.

From the Android app

  1. Open Camping Morocco and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Account section.
  3. Choose Delete account and follow the confirmation instructions.

From the website

Use the secure account-deletion request form on our production backend:

Open account deletion form

Depending on account type, the form may require confirmation using the credentials associated with your account. Do not send your password by email.

What is deleted?

Account deletion removes sign-in identity and associated server-side contributions, including reviews, reports, uploaded photos and pending corrections. Campsite facts already accepted into the public directory may remain in anonymized form. Locally saved favorites or settings may remain until you clear app storage or uninstall.

If you cannot access the form, contact bisapp.ink@outlook.com for assistance without sending passwords.
